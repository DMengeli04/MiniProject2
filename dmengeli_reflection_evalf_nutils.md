# Reflection: evalf_nutils

The activity history for evalf_nutils is irregular but highly active overall. Its longest zero-commit period lasted only one month, and it had no inactivity gaps of at least three months. Because of this, the short pause does not provide strong evidence of project abandonment or a meaningful interruption in development. It is better interpreted as a normal fluctuation in an otherwise active project.

Development continued immediately afterward with performance improvements, compatibility changes, dependency-related changes, and other maintenance work. Returning contributors, including Gertjan van Zwieten, remained involved. Recent activity before the September 2025 snapshot was dominated by bug fixes, including fixes to solver tolerances, removed functions, and lock contention, along with a Dependabot dependency update.

The project was Active as of September 30, 2025. In this case, there is not a substantial inactivity event requiring a complicated recovery explanation. The timeline instead illustrates how even an actively maintained project can have short periods with no commits without indicating that development has stopped.
