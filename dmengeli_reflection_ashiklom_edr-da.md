# Reflection: ashiklom_edr-da

The activity timeline for ashiklom_edr-da shows a declining pattern with three inactivity gaps of at least three months. Its longest gap lasted 49 months, from May 2021 through May 2025. Immediately before this gap, the commit history was focused heavily on research and manuscript-related work, including updating references, responding to reviewer comments, revising the discussion and conclusions, and updating acknowledgments. This makes it reasonable to hypothesize that development slowed after this phase of the research project was largely completed, although the commit messages do not explicitly state why the project became inactive.

The project technically recovered from its longest gap in June 2025, but the recovery was very limited. Only one post-gap commit appears in the collected data, and that commit simply created a LICENSE file. The commit was made by Alexey Shiklomanov, who was also responsible for the work immediately before the gap, although his recorded email address had changed. This suggests that the recovery came from the same contributor rather than a new group of developers.

As of the September 30, 2025 snapshot, the project is classified as Active because the June 2025 commit occurred within the previous six months. However, the timeline and nature of that commit suggest that the project is not experiencing the same level of active development that it had before the long gap. The project therefore demonstrates an important distinction between meeting the assignment's definition of an active project and showing a substantial recovery in development activity.
