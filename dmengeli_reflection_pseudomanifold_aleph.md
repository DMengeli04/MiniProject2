# Reflection: pseudomanifold_aleph

The activity timeline for pseudomanifold_aleph shows a declining pattern with five inactivity gaps of at least three months. Its longest gap lasted 29 months, from February 2023 through June 2025. Before this gap, commits included bug fixes, additions to persistence-diagram statistics, tests, a small data-structure implementation, and tutorial improvements. The commit history does not explicitly explain the long inactivity period, making the cause difficult to determine with confidence.

The project technically recovered in July 2025, but the recovery was very limited. Only one post-gap commit appears in the collected data, and it fixed a PKGBUILD URL. Bastian Rieck appears as a contributor both before and after the gap despite a change in recorded email address, indicating that the same maintainer returned rather than a new group taking over development.

The July 2025 commit makes the project Active under the assignment's September 2025 six-month rule. However, the 29-month gap followed by a single maintenance fix supports the broader declining pattern visible in the timeline. As with ashiklom_edr-da, its formal Active classification should therefore not be interpreted as evidence of a major return to sustained development.
